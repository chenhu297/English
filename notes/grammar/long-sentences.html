长难句的综合运用 · 断开 + 简化

第三部分 · 特殊句式和长难句  |  田静 第十二讲《长难句的综合运用》

🎓 全课收官

这是整门课最后一讲，没有新语法——而是把前面所有招式反过来用：不是"造"句，是"拆"句。再长再吓人的句子，都逃不出两步：断开 → 简化。

一句话抓本质：长难句 = 简单句 + 简单句 + …，再各自加了一堆修饰。倒着走回去就好：

长难句多个句子/多件事
← 断开 ←
标点·连接词
简单句一个句子/一件事
← 简化 ←
去修饰·找核心
核心一件事的主干

1两步法 · 断开 + 简化

第一步：断开 —— 把长句切成几个简单句

找两种"剪刀":

  • 标点：逗号 , · 分号 ; · 冒号 : · 破折号 —
  • 连接词：并列连词(and / but / or / so)、从属连词(that / whether、which / who、when / because / if / although / while)

第二步：简化 —— 每个简单句去修饰、抓主干

把这些"修饰包"整块划掉，剩下的主谓(宾)就是核心意思：

  • 形容词 / 副词、介词短语(in…, of…, with…)
  • 非谓语(doing / done / to do 的修饰)、定语从句
  • 插入语 / 同位语(成对逗号或破折号中间的)

2实战拆解 · 一步步来

讲义真题(剑 9)。看着长，两步拆完就清楚了：

He was preparing for university, when he had an accident that put an end to his sports career.

① 断开（在 , when 和 that 处切）：

分段是什么
He was preparing for university主句
when he had an accident时间状语从句
that put an end to his sports career定语从句(修饰 accident)

② 简化（每段抓主干）：He was preparing ／ he had an accident ／ (accident) put an end to his career。

读懂了

他在准备上大学，那时出了一场事故，这事故结束了他的运动生涯。—— 三个简单句串起来，长句秒懂。

再来一句更长的(剑 10)

Tourists flock to wells in far-flung corners of north-western India to gaze in wonder at these architectural marvels from hundreds of years ago, which serve as a reminder of both the ingenuity and artistry of ancient civilisations and of the value of water to human existence.

断开(在 , which 处)+ 简化(灰色修饰全划掉)：

→ Tourists flock to wells (to gaze at marvels)　·　which serve as a reminder。

核心：游客涌向这些井观赏，它们是一种提醒。其余全是"在哪、什么样、提醒什么"的修饰细节。

📝 自测 · 田静课后练习(用"断开 + 简化"分析)

1. People had become more environmentally conscious, and the Danish experiment had proved that bike-sharing was a real possibility.(剑 14)

断开：and(并列)、that(宾语从句)。两件事：① People had become conscious ② the experiment had proved [that bike-sharing was possible]。

2. The simplest explanation for this is that the axolotl lineage alone lost the ability to metamorphose, while others retained it.(剑 9)

断开：that(表语从句)、while(对比状语从句)。主干：The explanation is [that the lineage lost the ability]，while others retained it。

3. Alternatively, money could be used as the standard for quantification: if two different goods have the same price, it can be claimed that they produce the same quantity of pleasure.(剑 13)

断开：冒号 :、if(条件从句)、that(主语从句)。主干：money could be used as the standard；(条件)if goods have the same price → it can be claimed [that they produce the same pleasure]。

4.（工作长句）Although the migration was risky, we rolled it out in stages, so that any issue that appeared could be rolled back quickly.

断开：Although(让步)、so that(目的)、that(定语从句)。主干：we rolled it out in stages。让步=虽然有风险；目的=以便任何(出现的)问题都能快速回滚。
